# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

This project expects professional, practical collaboration. Participants should
help keep discussions focused on secure VPS operations, clear tradeoffs, and
reproducible evidence.

Examples of encouraged behavior:

- Be precise about risks, assumptions, and tested environments.
- Challenge technical claims with evidence and respect.
- Keep feedback actionable.
- Avoid exposing other people's credentials, infrastructure details, or private
  operational data.
- Make room for maintainers to prioritize safety over feature velocity.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private keys, tokens, server passwords, private IP inventories, or
  other sensitive material.
- Encouraging unauthorized access to systems or Tailnets.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions after maintainer direction.
- Spam, promotional noise, or unrelated platform advocacy.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues; reject
contributions; or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its
users.

When possible, maintainers will explain moderation decisions. Severe or
security-sensitive incidents may be handled without public detail.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the maintainer through a private channel. Security
issues should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
